What makes a Hop Along book different

A color key kids can read

Every page shows the number, a color swatch and the color name written out - Red, Blue, Green, Brown. No guessing what a dot means, and no odd shades you don't own.

Really made for ages 3-5

5 levels, easy to harder. Level 1 has 3 colors and spaces about an inch wide. Even the busiest Level 5 scenes keep every space about half an inch wide or bigger. We measure every page.

One picture per page

Pictures are printed on one side only, so markers can't ruin the next picture. Behind each one: a read-aloud fact, a color test strip, a "How did you feel?" face and a box to draw your own.

102 pages, 8.5 x 11 in

45 pictures, 45 activity pages and full answer pictures at the back. Big pages for little hands.
