Why moms keep these in the car

She can read the key herself

Every number has a swatch and the color's name - Red, Blue, Green. No squinting at a tiny dot, and no shades you don't own. For Sky Blue, any light blue crayon works.

Big enough for a fist full of crayon

Level 1 spaces are about an inch wide. Even the busiest Level 5 scene keeps every space around half an inch. We measure every page so she stays inside the lines on her own.

Flip it over, there's more

Pictures are printed on one side, so markers can't ruin the next one. Behind each picture: a fact to read aloud, a strip to test colors, a face to circle, and a box to draw her own.
